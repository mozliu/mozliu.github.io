  • Financial reporting: I strive to develop clear reporting with you so that you can understand the account your money tells…

  • Tax filings: 1099s, 990s, and other annual filings. I offer tax services for non-bookkeeping clients under the auspices of Full Harvest Taxes.

I charge $70/hr for all services. My intake process is that I meet with you for 30 min to an hour to get a sense of your needs. If it seems like a good fit, I write up an assessment for you outlining what services I think would be appropriate for you, along with an estimate of how much it would cost you to get them from me.
